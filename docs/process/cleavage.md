# Cleavage

## Peptide Release from Resin

## Purpose

1. **Release** the peptide from the solid support
2. **Remove** side-chain protecting groups (global deprotection)

---

## Standard Cleavage Cocktail

### TFA-Based Cocktail

| Component | Function | Typical % |
|---|---|---|
| Trifluoroacetic acid (TFA) | Acidolysis of ester/amide bonds | 90–95% |
| TIS (triisopropylsilane) | Carbocation scavenger | 2–5% |
| Water | Polar carbocation scavenger | 2–5% |
| EDT or DTT | For Met, Cys, Trp containing peptides | 0–2% |

### Recipe for Standard Peptides
```
TFA (95%) + TIS (2.5%) + H₂O (2.5%)
```

### Recipe for Cys/Met/Trp-Containing Peptides
```
TFA (94%) + TIS (2%) + H₂O (2%) + EDT (1%) + Phenol (1%)
```

---

## Procedure

### Step-by-Step

1. **Prepare cocktail** fresh (do not store TFA cocktails)
2. **Cool to 0 °C** in ice bath
3. **Add to resin** (10–20 mL/g of resin)
4. **Stir 1.5–4 hours** at room temperature (time depends on sequence)
5. **Filter** to remove resin beads (glass frit or PE frit)
6. **Wash resin** with small amount of neat TFA
7. **Precipitate** in cold diethyl ether or MTBE (10:1 ratio ether:filtrate)
8. **Centrifuge or filter** to collect precipitate
9. **Wash × 2–3** with cold ether
10. **Dry** under nitrogen stream or vacuum

---

## Cleavage Time Guidelines

| Protecting Groups | Time |
|---|---|
| Standard (no special groups) | 1.5–2 h |
| Arg(Pbf) present | 2.5–3 h |
| Trp(Boc) present | 2–3 h |
| Cysteine (Trt) present | 2–3 h |
| Cys(Acm) present | Keep ≤2 h (Acm is stable) |

---

## Precipitation

### Cold Ether Method
- **Ether**: diethyl ether or MTBE
- **Temperature**: 0 °C (ice bath)
- **Ratio**: 8–10 volumes ether per volume cleavage solution
- **Time**: 5–15 minutes precipitation
- **Result**: White to off-white solid

### Centrifugation (Small Scale)
- 3000–5000 × g for 5 minutes
- Decant supernatant
- Resuspend in fresh cold ether
- Repeat × 2

### Filtration (Larger Scale)
- Use glass frit filter (M or F porosity)
- Wash filter cake with ether
- Suction dry

## What Safety Precautions Are Needed for Cleavage?

| Hazard | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| TFA | Corrosive, toxic | Fume hood, gloves, goggles |
| TIS | Flammable | No open flames |
| Ether | Highly flammable, peroxide former | Use fresh, no sparks |
| EDT | Strong odor, toxic | Fume hood, minimize use |
