# Lyophilization

## Freeze-Drying of Peptides

## Purpose

Convert liquid peptide solution into stable dry powder by freezing and sublimation under vacuum.

---

## Lyophilization Process

``` mermaid

    A[Peptide Solution] --> B[Freezing];

```

---

## Step 1: Freezing

### Objective
Convert liquid water to ice, concentrating the peptide in a glassy or crystalline matrix.

### Parameters
| Parameter | Typical Value |
|---|---|
| Freeze temperature | -40 to -80 °C |
| Freeze rate | 0.5–2 °C/min |
| Annealing | Optional, -20 °C for 1–2 h |

### Considerations
- **Fast freezing**: Smaller ice crystals, larger surface area
- **Slow freezing**: Larger crystals, faster primary drying
- **Annealing**: Promotes ice crystal growth, reduces drying time

---

## Step 2: Primary Drying

### Objective
Sublime ice directly to vapor under vacuum.

### Parameters
| Parameter | Typical Value |
|---|---|
| Chamber pressure | 0.1–0.5 mbar |
| Shelf temperature | -20 to -10 °C |
| Product temperature | Maintain below collapse temp |
| Duration | 12–48 hours |

### Collapse Temperature
- Typically -20 to -40 °C for peptides
- Varies with formulation (bulking agents raise collapse temp)
- Critical quality parameter — product must not melt

### Endpoint Determination
- Product temperature approaches shelf temperature
- Pressure rise test (Pirani vs. capacitance manometer)

---

## Step 3: Secondary Drying

### Objective
Remove bound water from the peptide cake.

### Parameters
| Parameter | Typical Value |
|---|---|
| Shelf temperature | 20–40 °C |
| Pressure | ≤0.05 mbar |
| Duration | 4–12 hours |

### Target Residual Moisture
- Peptides: <5% (manufacturing spec)
- Desirable: <2% for long-term stability

---

## Lyophilizer Components

| Component | Function |
|---|---|
| Vacuum pump | Maintain low pressure |
| Refrigeration | Cool shelves and condenser |
| Condenser trap | Trap water vapor (-50 to -80 °C) |
| Shelf system | Heat/cool product temperature |
| Control system | Recipe programming and monitoring |

---

## Quality Considerations

### Cake Appearance
- **Acceptable**: Elegant, intact cake, white to off-white
- **Defects**: Cracking, shrinkage, melt-back, collapse

### Common Issues
| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Cake collapse | Temperature above collapse point | Slow ramp, lower temp |
| High residual moisture | Insufficient secondary drying | Extend secondary drying |
| Partial melting | Vacuum failure or pressure rise | Check seals, maintain vacuum |

---

## Storage After Lyophilization

- **Container**: Air-tight vials or bags with desiccant
- **Temperature**: -20 °C (long-term), 4 °C (short-term)
- **Protection**: Light-sensitive peptides require amber vials
- **Shelf life**: 2–5 years at -20 °C under dry conditions
