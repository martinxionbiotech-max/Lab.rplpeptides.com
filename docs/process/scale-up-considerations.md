# Scale-Up Considerations

## From Laboratory to Production Scale

## Key Scale-Up Challenges

| Challenge | Lab Scale | Production Scale | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heat transfer | Efficient | Slow, requires jackets | Exothermic reactions |
| Mixing | Homogeneous | Gradient possible | Incomplete reactions |
| Diffusion | Fast | Slower | Extended reaction times |
| Solvent handling | Small volumes | Large volumes, waste | Economics |
| HPLC throughput | Minutes per run | Hours per run | Bottleneck |

## How Is SPPS Scaled Up from Lab to Production?

### Resin Bed Considerations

| Parameter | Lab (1 g) | Pilot (100 g) | Production (5 kg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vessel volume | 10 mL | 1 L | 50 L |
| Resin bed height | 2–3 cm | 10–20 cm | 30–50 cm |
| Solvent per coupling | 10 mL | 1 L | 50 L |
| Mixing type | N₂ bubbling | Mechanical stirrer | Mechanical + recirculation |

### Flow Engineering

- **Resin settling**: Larger beds need stronger agitation
- **Channeling**: Can cause incomplete reagent penetration
- **Sampling**: Need representative in-process samples
- **Wash efficiency**: More washes needed at larger scale

## What Challenges Arise in Purification Scale-Up?

| Column ID | Max Load (crude) | Flow Rate | Run Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 mm | 500 mg | 30 mL/min | 30–60 min |
| 50 mm | 2 g | 80 mL/min | 30–60 min |
| 100 mm | 10 g | 300 mL/min | 40–80 min |
| 200 mm | 50 g | 1 L/min | 60–120 min |

### Multiple Column Approach
For >50 g, consider parallel columns or batch processing:

```
Column A: Purifying → Column B: Loading → Column C: Equilibrating
```

| Solvent cost share | 10–15% | 15–25% |
| HPLC consumables share | 20–30% | 10–15% |
| Labor share | 20–30% | 5–10% |
