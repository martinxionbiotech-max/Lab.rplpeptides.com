# Quality Control

Analytical methods and testing protocols for peptide characterization and release.

---

## QC Tests

- **[HPLC Analysis](../quality-control/hplc-analysis.md)** — Purity determination by reversed-phase HPLC
- **[LC-MS Testing](../quality-control/lc-ms-testing.md)** — Identity confirmation by mass spectrometry
- **[Purity Analysis](../quality-control/purity-analysis.md)** — HPLC purity calculation and impurity profiling
- **[Peptide Content](../quality-control/peptide-content.md)** — Net peptide content determination
- **[Water Content](../quality-control/water-content.md)** — Karl Fischer moisture analysis
- **[Residual Solvents](../quality-control/residual-solvents.md)** — GC analysis of organic solvents
- **[Mass Confirmation](../quality-control/mass-confirmation.md)** — Molecular weight verification
- **[Amino Acid Analysis](../quality-control/amino-acid-analysis.md)** — Composition verification
- **[Endotoxin Testing](../quality-control/endotoxin-testing.md)** — See quality specs at [RPL Peptide FAQ](https://rplpeptides.com/rpl-peptide-faq/)
- **[COA Understanding](../quality-control/coa-understanding.md)** — How to read a Certificate of Analysis
- **[Method Validation](../quality-control/method-validation.md)** — ICH Q2(R1) method validation for peptide testing
- **[Stability Testing](../quality-control/stability-testing.md)** — Shelf life and degradation studies
- **[Impurity Profiling](../quality-control/impurity-profiling.md)** — Identification and quantification of peptide impurities

## Typical QC Specification

| Test | Research Grade | GMP Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Purity (HPLC) | ≥95% | ≥98% |
| LC-MS | Confirm MW | Confirm MW |
| Peptide Content | ≥80% | ≥85% |
| Water | ≤5% | ≤5% |
| Residual Solvents | — | ≤0.5% |
| Endotoxin | — | ≤10 EU/mg |
| Appearance | White powder | White powder |
