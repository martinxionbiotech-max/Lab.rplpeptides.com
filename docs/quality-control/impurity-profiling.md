# Impurity Profiling

## Identification and Quantification of Peptide Impurities

## Types of Peptide Impurities

### Process-Related Impurities
| Impurity | Origin | HPLC Profile |
|---|---|---|
| Deletion sequences | Incomplete coupling | Earlier eluting |
| Truncated peptides | Failed deprotection | Earlier eluting |
| Insertion sequences | Double coupling | Later eluting |
| D-isomers | Racemization during activation | Co-eluting or shoulder |
| β-Asp peptides | Aspartimide formation | Earlier or later |

### Degradation-Related Impurities
| Impurity | Origin | Detection |
|---|---|---|
| Oxidation products | Air/stress | MS +16 Da |
| Deamidation | pH/temperature | MS +1 Da |
| Aggregates | Concentration/temperature | SEC-HPLC |
| Hydrolysis products | Acidic conditions | MS fragment |

## What Are Acceptable Single Impurity Limits?

| Impurity Level | Research Grade | GMP Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Any single impurity | ≤2.0% | ≤0.5–1.0% |
| Total impurities | ≤5.0% | ≤2.0% |
| Unknown impurity | ≤1.0% | ≤0.2% |

## Identification Confidence

| Confidence Level | Evidence Required |
|---|---|
| Tentative | Retention time + mass match |
| Confirmed | Retention time + MS/MS fragmentation |
| Definitive | Co-elution with reference standard |
