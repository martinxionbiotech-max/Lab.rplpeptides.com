# Amino Acid Analysis

## Peptide Composition Verification

## Principle

Amino acid analysis (AAA) hydrolyzes the peptide into individual amino acids and quantifies each one, confirming the correct composition.

---

## Method

1. **Hydrolysis**: 6 M HCl, 110 °C, 24 hours
2. **Derivatization**: Pre- or post-column
3. **Separation**: HPLC or UPLC
4. **Detection**: UV or fluorescence

---

## What It Confirms

| Check | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Amino acid ratios | Match theoretical ratios |
| Absence of unexpected AAs | No contamination |
| Overall composition | Correct peptide |

## What Are the Limitations of Amino Acid Analysis?

- Trp destroyed by acid hydrolysis
- Cys needs special derivatization
- Asn/Gln converted to Asp/Glu
