# Peptide Content

## Net Peptide Content Determination

## Principle

Peptide content measures the actual amount of peptide in the final product powder, accounting for:
- Water content
- Residual solvents
- Counter-ions (TFA, acetate)
- Salts

---

## Calculation

```
Peptide Content (%) = 100% - (Water% + Residual Solvents% + Counter-ion%)
```

### Typical Results

| Component | Research Grade | GMP Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Net peptide content | ≥80% | ≥85% |
| Water | ≤5% | ≤5% |
| TFA counter-ion | 5–15% | 5–15% |
| Residual solvents | — | ≤0.5% |

---

## Why Content Matters

- **Dosing accuracy**: Lower content = less active peptide per mg
- **Comparability**: Consistent batches require consistent content
- **Formulation**: Excipient calculations depend on net peptide
