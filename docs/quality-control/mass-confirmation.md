# Mass Confirmation

## Molecular Weight Verification

## Principle

Mass confirmation uses mass spectrometry to verify that the peptide's molecular weight matches the theoretical value calculated from its amino acid sequence.

---

## How It Works

| Ion | m/z (for a 1000 Da peptide) |
|---|---|
| [M+H]⁺ | 1001.5 |
| [M+2H]²⁺ | 501.3 |
| [M+3H]³⁺ | 334.5 |
| Deconvoluted mass | 1000.5 Da |

---

## Expected vs. Actual

A mass match within ±1 Da confirms correct peptide identity.
Mismatches indicate:
- ±1 AA mass: deletion or insertion
- ±18 Da: dehydration
- ±16 Da: oxidation (Met, Trp)
- +113 Da: TFA adduct

## Report Format

| Parameter | Result |
|---|---|
| Theoretical MW | 1234.56 Da |
| Found MW (LC-MS) | 1234.45 Da |
| Mass error | -0.11 Da |
| Identity | ✅ Confirmed |
