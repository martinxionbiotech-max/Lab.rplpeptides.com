# Water Content

## Karl Fischer Moisture Analysis

## Principle

Karl Fischer titration measures water content by coulometric or volumetric titration. For peptides, coulometric KF is preferred due to small sample size and low water content.

---

## Method

- **Sample size**: 5–20 mg
- **Method**: Coulometric Karl Fischer
- **Solvent**: Hydranal or equivalent
- **Detection**: Amperometric endpoint

---

## What Are Water Content Acceptance Criteria?

| Grade | Water Content |
|---|---|
| Research | ≤5% |
| Purified | ≤5% |
| GMP | ≤5% (target <2%) |
| Lyophilized | ≤3% |

---

## Why Water Matters

- **Stability**: High moisture accelerates degradation
- **Content calculation**: Must be subtracted for net peptide content
- **Microbial growth**: >10% moisture can support growth
