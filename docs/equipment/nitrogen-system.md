# Nitrogen System

## Inert Gas Supply

## Category
Synthesis Support

## Application
Inert atmosphere for SPPS reactions

## Function
Provide dry nitrogen to maintain inert headspace above reaction vessels, preventing oxidation and moisture ingress.

---

## Key Parameters

| Parameter | Typical Range |
|---|---|
| Supply pressure | 1–5 bar |
| Purity | 99.5–99.999% |
| Flow rate | 1–50 L/min (per vessel) |
| Dew point | -40 to -60 °C |

---

## Applications in Peptide Synthesis

- Resin swelling
- Sparging during coupling reactions
- Inert headspace during deprotection
- Transfer of solvents
- Drying of final product
