# Prep HPLC Scale-Up

## Scaling Peptide Purification from Lab to Production

## Column Scaling Principles

### Linear Scale-Up
The standard approach: keep column length and linear velocity constant, increase column diameter.

| Parameter | Lab (10 mm) | Pilot (50 mm) | Production (100 mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cross-section area | 0.79 cm² | 19.6 cm² | 78.5 cm² |
| Scale factor | 1× | 25× | 100× |
| Flow rate | 5 mL/min | 125 mL/min | 500 mL/min |
| Max load (crude) | 20 mg | 500 mg | 2 g |

### Particle Size Selection

| Particle Size | Efficiency | Pressure | Load Capacity | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 μm | Highest | High | Low | Analytical |
| 10 μm | High | Medium | Medium | Lab prep |
| 15–20 μm | Good | Low | High | Process |
| 30 μm | Moderate | Very low | Very high | Production |

---

## How to Transfer a Gradient from Analytical to Prep Scale?

To transfer a gradient from analytical to preparative scale:

1. Use same **column volumes** for gradient
2. Keep **linear velocity** constant
3. Sample loading: scale by **column volume × sample concentration**

## What Are Production-Scale HPLC Considerations?

| Factor | Consideration |
|---|---|
| Column hardware | Dynamic axial compression (DAC) for >50 mm |
| Solvent consumption | 10–50 L per batch (recycle where possible) |
| Fraction collection | Automated peak-based collection |
| Product recovery | 70–85% typical for single pass |
| Re-purification | Sometimes needed for >98% purity |
