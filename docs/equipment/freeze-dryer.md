# Freeze Dryer (Lyophilizer)

## Peptide Drying Equipment

## Category
Drying Equipment

## Application
Remove solvents from purified peptide solutions to produce stable powder

## Function
Freeze peptide solution and sublime ice under vacuum.

---

## Key Parameters

| Parameter | Research Scale | Process Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Condenser temperature | -50 to -80 °C | -50 to -80 °C |
| Shelf temperature range | -40 to +60 °C | -40 to +60 °C |
| Vacuum level | 0.01–1.0 mbar | 0.01–1.0 mbar |
| Shelf area | 0.1–1.0 m² | 1–20 m² |
| Ice capacity | 2–20 kg | 20–500 kg |

## Types

| Type | Capacity | Best For |
