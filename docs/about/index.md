# About This Documentation

## RPL Peptides Lab — Technical Documentation

This site serves as a technical knowledge base for peptide manufacturing, covering the complete lifecycle from solid-phase peptide synthesis through purification, quality control, and analytical testing.

---

## Why Does This Technical Documentation Exist?

- Provide detailed technical information about peptide manufacturing processes
- Help researchers and procurement professionals understand quality parameters
- Serve as a reference for equipment specifications and raw material standards
- Support procurement decisions with authoritative technical content

---

## What Does the Documentation Cover?

The content covers:

- **Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS)**: Complete process documentation
- **Equipment**: Specifications and applications for manufacturing and analytical equipment
- **Raw Materials**: Quality parameters for amino acids, resins, solvents, and reagents
- **Quality Control**: Analytical methods, acceptance criteria, and COA interpretation
- **Process Flows**: Visual workflow diagrams

---

## Content Philosophy

All content is written as evergreen technical documentation. Procedures, parameters, and specifications are based on established industry practices and are reviewed annually.
