# Production Scale


## TL;DR

Peptide production scales from laboratory (mg–g), through pilot (10–200 g), to commercial (200 g–kg+). Scale-up requires careful heat transfer, mixing, and HPLC throughput planning.

## Research Scale (mg to gram)

| Parameter | Typical Range |
|---|---|
| Resin quantity | 0.1–5 g |
| Vessel size | 10–100 mL |
| Synthesizer | Bench-top automated |
| Purification | Semi-prep HPLC |
| Facility | Standard lab |

Best suited for: Early research, screening, small animal studies

---

## Preclinical Scale (grams to 10 g)

| Parameter | Typical Range |
|---|---|
| Resin quantity | 5–50 g |
| Vessel size | 100–500 mL |
| Synthesizer | Mid-scale automated |
| Purification | Prep HPLC (30 mm column) |
| Facility | Controlled environment |

Best suited for: Toxicology studies, PK/PD, formulation development

---

## Clinical Scale (10 g to 100 g)

| Parameter | Typical Range |
|---|---|
| Resin quantity | 50–500 g |
| Vessel size | 0.5–5 L |
| Synthesizer | Large-scale automated |
| Purification | Prep HPLC (50–100 mm column) |
| Facility | GMP or GMP-like |

Best suited for: Phase I–III clinical trials

---

## Commercial Scale (>100 g to multi-kg)

| Parameter | Typical Range |
|---|---|
| Resin quantity | 0.5–50 kg |
| Vessel size | 5–200 L |
| Purification | Multiple columns or continuous |
| Facility | Full GMP |

Best suited for: Approved drugs, commercial supply

---

## Scale-Up Considerations

| Factor | Challenge | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Heat transfer | Exothermic reactions harder to control | Jacketed vessels, controlled addition |
| Mixing efficiency | Inhomogeneous resin suspension | Optimized agitator design |
| Diffusion | Slower in large resin beds | Extended reaction times |
| HPLC throughput | Long purification times | Larger columns, higher flow rates |
| Waste handling | Large solvent volumes | Solvent recovery systems |
