## Core Topics

### Solid Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS)

SPPS is the dominant method for commercial peptide production. Key stages include:

1. **Resin Selection** — Choosing the solid support based on C-terminal amino acid
2. **Coupling** — Sequential addition of protected amino acids
3. **Deprotection** — Removal of temporary protecting groups
4. **Cleavage** — Release of peptide from resin
5. **Purification** — Preparative HPLC to achieve target purity
6. **Lyophilization** — Freeze-drying to stable powder form
