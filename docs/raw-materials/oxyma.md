# Oxyma — Coupling Additive

## Safer Alternative to HOBt

## Definition

Oxyma (ethyl 2-cyano-2-(hydroxyimino)acetate) is used as an additive with carbodiimide activators to suppress racemization and improve coupling efficiency.

---

## Specifications

| Parameter | Typical Value |
|---|---|
| Purity | ≥99% |
| Appearance | White to off-white powder |
| Storage | 2–8 °C |
| CAS number | 3849-21-6 |

---

## Advantages Over HOBt

| Feature | Oxyma | HOBt |
|---|---|---|
| Explosion hazard | None | Shock-sensitive at scale |
| Coupling efficiency | Comparable | Standard |
| Racemization suppression | Excellent | Good |
| Cost | Moderate | Low |

---

## Typical Use

- With DIC (3 eq Oxyma + 3 eq DIC)
- In DMF or DMF/DCM mixtures
- 30–60 minute coupling time

---

## Application in SPPS

Oxyma is widely adopted as the safer alternative to HOBt for large-scale and GMP peptide manufacturing.
