# Raw Material Supplier Qualification

## Evaluating and Qualifying Peptide Synthesis Raw Materials

## Why Supplier Qualification Matters

Raw material quality directly impacts:
- **Coupling efficiency** (low purity → deletions)
- **Final product quality** (impurities carry through)
- **Batch reproducibility** (consistent supplier = consistent product)
- **Regulatory compliance** (GMP requires qualified suppliers)

---

## What Are the Raw Material Qualification Levels?

| Level | Requirements | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1: Screening** | COA review, appearance check | Initial evaluation |
| **Level 2: Incoming QC** | Identity, purity, water testing | Every batch |
| **Level 3: Full Qualification** | Full protocol, impurity profile | GMP raw materials |
| **Level 4: Audit** | On-site supplier audit | Critical suppliers |

---

## Key Quality Parameters by Material

### Amino Acids
| Test | Method | Acceptable |
|---|---|---|
| HPLC purity | RP-HPLC | ≥99.0% |
| Chiral purity | Chiral HPLC | ≤0.1% D-enantiomer |
| Water | Karl Fischer | ≤0.5% |
| Residual solvents | GC | ≤0.1% |

### Resins
| Test | Method | Acceptable |
|---|---|---|
| Loading capacity | UV Fmoc test | ±10% of labeled |
| Bead size distribution | Sieve/microscope | 100–200 mesh |
| Swelling | Gravimetric | DMF 4–6 mL/g |

### Solvents
| Test | Method | Acceptable |
|---|---|---|
| Purity | GC | ≥99.8% |
| Water | Karl Fischer | ≤0.05% |
| UV cutoff | Spectrophotometer | Per specification |

## COA Verification Checklist

1. ✅ Product name matches PO
2. ✅ Batch number present and traceable
3. ✅ Test results within specification
4. ✅ Expiry/retest date valid
5. ✅ Authorized signature present
6. ✅ Analytical method referenced

---

## Supplier Audit Red Flags

| Red Flag | Risk |
|---|---|
| No batch traceability | Contamination can't be traced |
| Re-testing old material | Material may be expired |
| Refusing COA disclosure | Inconsistent quality |
| Frequent specification changes | Process not validated |
