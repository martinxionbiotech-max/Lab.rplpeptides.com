# Rink Amide Resin

## Solid Support for C-Terminal Amide Peptides

## Definition

Rink amide resin produces peptides with C-terminal amide (-CONH₂), which is the native form for many bioactive peptides.

---

## Specifications

| Parameter | Typical Value |
|---|---|
| Base polymer | Crosslinked polystyrene |
| Loading | 0.3–0.8 mmol/g |
| Linker | Rink amide (4-[(2,4-dimethoxyphenyl)(Fmoc-amino)methyl]phenoxy) |
| Swelling (DMF) | 4–6 mL/g |

---

## Key Feature

The Rink amide linker incorporates a pre-attached Fmoc-protected amine group. Upon TFA cleavage, the peptide is released with a C-terminal amide.

---

## Cleavage

- **Reagent**: TFA (90–95%)
- **Product**: Peptide-CONH₂ (C-terminal amide)
- **Time**: 1.5–3 hours

---

## Why C-Terminal Amide Matters

Many naturally occurring peptides (and therapeutic peptides) have C-terminal amidation, which:
- Increases resistance to carboxypeptidases
- Enhances biological activity
- Alters peptide charge and solubility

---

## Common Peptides Requiring Amide

- GLP-1 analogs (semaglutide, liraglutide)
- Melanotan II
- Thymosin alpha-1
- Most therapeutic peptides >10 AA
