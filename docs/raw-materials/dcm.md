# DCM — Dichloromethane

## Resin Swelling and Wash Solvent

## Definition

DCM (dichloromethane) is used in SPPS primarily for resin swelling and as a wash solvent.

---

## Specifications

| Parameter | Typical Value |
|---|---|
| Purity | ≥99.8% |
| Water content | ≤0.02% |
| Grade | Synthesis grade |

---

## Role in SPPS

| Function | Detail |
|---|---|
| Resin swelling | Best swelling for polystyrene resins (6–8 mL/g) |
| Pre-swelling | Before first coupling in DMF |
| Wash | After DMF washes, before DMF again |
| Cleavage cocktail component | Diluent for TFA |

---

## Handling

| Hazard | Precaution |
|---|---|
| Toxic (suspected carcinogen) | Fume hood |
| Irritant | Gloves, goggles |
| Volatile | Keep container sealed |
