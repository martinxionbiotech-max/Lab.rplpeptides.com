# Boc Amino Acids

## Boc-Protected Amino Acids

## Definition

Boc (tert-butyloxycarbonyl) protected amino acids are used in Boc-SPPS, an alternative strategy to Fmoc chemistry.

---

## How Do Boc Amino Acids Compare with Fmoc?

| Feature | Boc Strategy | Fmoc Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Protecting group | Boc | Fmoc |
| Deprotection | TFA (strong acid) | Piperidine (base) |
| Final cleavage | HF or TFMSA (very strong acid) | TFA |
| Equipment | Acid-resistant required | Standard glass |
| Current usage | Rare (specialized) | Most common |

---

## Applications

Boc chemistry is still used for:
- Peptides requiring special side-chain protection
- Historical peptide sequences
- Specific research applications
