# Fmoc Amino Acids


## TL;DR

Fmoc amino acids are the building blocks of SPPS, requiring ≥99% HPLC purity and ≤0.1% D-enantiomer content. Proper storage at -20 °C prevents degradation.

## Definition

Fmoc (9-fluorenylmethoxycarbonyl) protected amino acids are the primary building blocks for Fmoc-SPPS. The Fmoc group protects the α-amino group during coupling cycles.

---

## Structure

- Fmoc-NH-CHR-COOH
- R = side chain (varies by amino acid)
- Side chains may also be protected

---

## What Quality Parameters Define Fmoc Amino Acids?

| Parameter | Typical Spec | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Purity (HPLC) | ≥99% | RP-HPLC |
| Optical purity | ≤0.1% D-enantiomer | Chiral HPLC |
| Water content | ≤0.5% | Karl Fischer |
| Residual solvents | ≤0.1% | GC |
| Free amine | ≤0.1% | Kaiser test |
| Appearance | White to off-white powder | Visual |

---

## How Should Fmoc Amino Acids Be Stored?

- **Temperature**: -20 °C
- **Moisture**: Desiccated (hydroscopic)
- **Light**: Protected from UV
- **Shelf life**: 2 years at -20 °C

---

## Application in SPPS

1. Activator converts Fmoc-AA-OH to active ester
2. Active ester couples to free N-terminus on growing peptide
3. Piperidine removes Fmoc group after coupling
4. Next Fmoc-AA-OH is added
