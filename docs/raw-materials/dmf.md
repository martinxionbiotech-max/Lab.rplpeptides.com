# DMF — Dimethylformamide

## Primary SPPS Solvent

## Definition

DMF (dimethylformamide) is the most common solvent in Fmoc-SPPS. It is used for resin swelling, coupling reactions, washes, and deprotection.

---

## Specifications

| Parameter | Typical Value |
|---|---|
| Purity | ≥99.8% |
| Water content | ≤0.05% |
| Grade | Synthesis grade or HPLC grade |

---

## Role in SPPS

| Function | Detail |
|---|---|
| Resin swelling | Swells polystyrene resin 4–6 mL/g |
| Coupling solvent | Dissolves Fmoc-amino acids and activators |
| Wash solvent | Removes excess reagents between steps |
| Deprotection | Carrier for piperidine (20%) |

---

## Handling & Safety

| Hazard | Precaution |
|---|---|
| Toxic (teratogen) | Use fume hood |
| Absorbed through skin | Wear nitrile gloves |
| Flammable | Keep away from ignition sources |
| Hygroscopic | Seal tightly, use fresh |
